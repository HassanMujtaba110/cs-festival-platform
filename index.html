RULES & FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can I participate in more than one competition?

Yes, provided their competition times do not collide. For instance, you can participate in CodeSprint on Day 1 and RoboClash on Day 3. Simultaneous round collisions are disallowed.

What do I need to bring on the festival day?

Every registered attendee must show their official student university ID card along with their digital or printed CSF '26 Festival Pass (with QR code) for lanyard clearance.

Are cross-department or mixed-semester squads allowed?

Yes! Squad members for HackMatrix, CyberSiege, and RoboClash may be from different semesters or computer science specializations (CS, SE, AI, DS, Cyber).
